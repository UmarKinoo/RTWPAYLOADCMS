Candidate Registration & Dashboard

What candidates provide at signup, and what they can add or edit on their dashboard
Prepared for
ReadyToWork
Client
Scope
Website registration wizard + candidate dashboard
Does not include ESCO or ReadyBot flows
Legend
* = Required to continue / submit
Optional = can be left blank

Candidates complete a 6-step registration on the website, then are redirected to their dashboard. Required fields must be filled before moving to the next step or submitting. After signup they can edit most of their profile and add extra items (photo, bio, education, CV, preferences, benefits).

Part B — After registration (candidate dashboard)

After signup, candidates land on their dashboard. They can update information from registration and add new profile sections below.

New items they can add (not asked at signup)

Section What they can add Required?
Profile Profile photo (JPEG / PNG / WebP / GIF, max 5MB) Optional
About me Bio / about text (up to 2,000 characters) Optional
Education Multiple entries. Per entry: Degree/Certification (*), Institution (*), Field of study (optional), Graduation year (optional), Description (optional) Section optional; * required when saving an entry
Job preferences Preferred job title, preferred location, preferred salary, work type (full-time / part-time / contract / freelance / any), shift preference (day / night / rotating / any) Optional
Job benefits Preferred benefits (e.g. health insurance, accommodation, transportation, annual leave, end of service, training, performance bonus, overtime pay, meal allowance, other). Multiple benefits can be added. Optional
Resume / CV Upload or replace CV (PDF / DOC / DOCX, max 10MB); download or remove Optional

Editable from registration (already collected at signup)
